## Day 1: Guwahati to Shillong
- **Guwahati Airport/Railway Station** — Land in Guwahati and rent a car or hire a taxi to drive down to Shillong.
- **Umiam Lake** — A massive, scenic reservoir on the way to Shillong. Stop by for beautiful views and speedboating.
- **Shillong** — Check into your accommodation and stay overnight in the capital city.

## Day 2: Trekking in Kyntaiai Valley
- **Kyntaiai Valley** — Embark on a scenic 1.5-hour trek through rolling green hills and meadows. Totally worth the hike.
- **Mawkyrwat** — Head to this scenic region and stay overnight.

## Day 3: Split Rock & Mawlynbna Water Adventures
- **Split Rock** — Explore a giant rock split into two pathways due to an ancient earthquake. A thrilling climb through narrow crevices.
- **Mawlynbna** — An adventure hub perfect for water activities like cliff jumping, floating, kayaking, and river canyoning.
- **Shillong** — Drive back to Shillong for an overnight stay.

## Day 4: Laitlum Canyon & WahRashi Waterfalls
- **Laitlum Canyon** — Also known as the 'End of the World', offering breathtaking panoramic views of deep gorges and valleys. Best visited during sunset.
- **WahRashi Waterfalls** — A stunning multi-tiered waterfall surrounded by green hills. Perfect for a long, refreshing swim in the natural pools.

## Day 5: Cherrapunji Waterfalls
- **Pung Iyer Waterfall** — Located in Cherrapunji, this hidden gem requires a 45-minute hike but rewards you with a stunning turquoise pool.
- **Cheerfully Falls** — An equally stunning waterfall where you can swim in crystal-clear waters surrounded by dense jungle.

## Day 6: Living Root Bridges & Iconic Plunge Falls
- **Umkar Living Root Bridge** — An iconic bridge grown from the roots of rubber trees. Choose this bridge if you want to avoid a long trek, or opt for the Double Decker Bridge if you prefer a challenging hike.
- **Kynrem Falls** — A spectacular three-tiered waterfall cascading down from a height of over 300 meters.
- **Seven Sister Waterfalls** — A wide, multi-segmented waterfall representing the seven states of Northeast India.
- **Nohkalikai Falls** — End your trip at one of India's tallest plunge waterfalls, featuring a dramatic drop into a deep green pool.

## Tips
- For living root bridges, choose Umkar Bridge if you want zero trekking, or Double Decker Bridge if you are up for a strenuous hike.
- Pack quick-dry clothing and water shoes as you will be swimming and doing water sports frequently.
- Hire a local driver/cab from Guwahati or Shillong for the entire trip, as navigating the mountainous terrain can be challenging.
