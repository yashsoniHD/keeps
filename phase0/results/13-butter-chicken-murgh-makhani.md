## Ingredients

**For the Marinade**
- 1/2 cup plain yoghurt, full fat
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp chilli powder or cayenne pepper powder
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tbsp ginger, freshly grated
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1.5 lb / 750 g chicken thigh fillets, cut into bite size pieces

**For the Sauce & Curry**
- 2 tbsp (30 g) ghee or butter (or 1 tbsp vegetable oil)
- 1 cup tomato passata (tomato puree)
- 1 cup heavy / thickened cream
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 1/4 tsp salt

**For Serving**
- Basmati rice or white rice
- Coriander/cilantro (optional)

## Method
1. Optional: For an extra smooth sauce, combine all marinade ingredients (except the chicken) in a food processor and blend until smooth.
2. Combine the marinade ingredients with the chicken in a bowl. Cover and refrigerate overnight, or up to 24 hours (minimum 3 hours).
3. Heat the ghee, butter, or oil over high heat in a large fry pan.
4. Remove the chicken from the marinade without wiping or shaking off the marinade (do not pour the remaining marinade from the bowl into the pan yet).
5. Place the chicken in the fry pan and cook for about 3 minutes, or until the chicken is white all over.
6. Add the tomato passata, cream, sugar, salt, and any remaining marinade left in the bowl to the pan.
7. Turn the heat down to low and simmer for 20 minutes. Taste and add more salt if needed.
8. Garnish with coriander/cilantro leaves and serve hot with basmati rice.

## Tips
- Marinate the chicken overnight (up to 24 hours) for the best flavor and tenderness.
- For a lighter version, this recipe skips the heavy restaurant practice of adding extra butter at the end, relying on cream for richness.
- To make quick, no-fry papadums, place them around the edge of a microwave turntable and cook on high for 45 to 60 seconds until puffed.
- Vegetarian substitutions: The sauce works beautifully with paneer, chickpeas, red kidney beans, or roasted zucchini.
