## 1. Portal - Cinematic Live Wallpapers
- Turns your Mac desktop into a live, cinematic nature scene featuring waterfalls, rainforests, and volcanic landscapes.
- Footage is shot in up to 12K resolution and paired with immersive spatial audio.
- Dynamic scenes sync with your local time zone, shifting seamlessly from morning to night.
- Includes a selection of completely free portals (e.g., Redwood National Park, Amazon Thunderstorm, Log Fire) with a Pro upgrade option for more variety.

## 2. Clocks - Animated Standby Mode
- Transforms your screen into a beautiful, animated clock display similar to iOS StandBy mode.
- Includes over 20 animated backgrounds and multiple clock styles to mix and match.
- Highly customizable layouts, fonts, and gradient paths to match your desk aesthetic.

## 3. Loop - Radial Window Management
- Reimagines window management on macOS using an intuitive radial menu.
- Triggered by holding a customizable shortcut key (like 'fn') and moving the cursor in the direction you want to snap the window.
- Completely free, lightweight, and open-source software.
