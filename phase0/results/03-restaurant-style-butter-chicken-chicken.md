# Restaurant-Style Butter Chicken (Chicken Makhani)

> **AI summary:** Learn how to make a rich and creamy restaurant-style Butter Chicken at home using a single pan for both frying the chicken and simmering the velvety tomato-cashew gravy. This classic recipe features marinated chicken chunks cooked to perfection and folded into a smooth, buttery sauce finished with fresh cream and dried fenugreek leaves.

## Ingredients

**For the Chicken Marinade**
- 300 g boneless chicken, cut into chunks
- 1 tsp garlic paste
- 1 tsp ginger paste
- 1 tsp red chili powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp oil (for pan frying)

**For the Gravy**
- 1 medium onion, roughly chopped
- 1 tbsp oil
- 3 tbsp butter, divided
- 4 medium tomatoes, roughly chopped
- 12 cashew nuts
- 1 cup water
- 1 tsp garlic paste
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp malt vinegar
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp garam masala powder
- 1 tbsp red chili powder
- 2 tbsp fresh cream
- 1 tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves), crushed

**For Garnish**
- 1 tbsp fresh cream
- 1 pinch kasuri methi, crushed

## Method
1. In a bowl, combine the chicken chunks with garlic paste, ginger paste, red chili powder, and salt. Mix well and marinate for 15 to 20 minutes.
2. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the marinated chicken pieces and pan-fry until cooked through and lightly browned. Transfer the chicken to a bowl and set aside.
3. In the same pan, add the chopped onion, a splash of oil, and a spoonful of butter. Sauté until the onions are lightly sweated.
4. Add the roughly chopped tomatoes and cashew nuts to the pan. Mix well.
5. Pour in the water, then add garlic paste, salt, malt vinegar, sugar, garam masala powder, and red chili powder. Stir to combine.
6. Simmer the tomato mixture over medium-low heat for 15 to 20 minutes until everything becomes soft and mushy.
7. Transfer the mixture to a deep bowl and blend using a hand blender until it forms a smooth, fine puree.
8. Strain the pureed gravy back into the same pan using a fine sieve, pressing well to extract all the liquid and discard the seeds and skin.
9. Reheat the strained gravy. Add the remaining butter, fresh cream, the cooked chicken pieces, and crushed kasuri methi.
10. Simmer the butter chicken for 5 to 7 minutes until the gravy thickens and coats the chicken.
11. Transfer to a serving bowl, garnish with a swirl of fresh cream and a sprinkle of crushed kasuri methi, and serve hot.

## Tips
- Straining the gravy is crucial to achieving the signature velvety, smooth 'makhani' texture.
- Malt vinegar adds a subtle tang that balances the sweetness of the sugar and tomatoes.
- You can substitute boneless chicken thighs for chicken breasts for juicier meat.
