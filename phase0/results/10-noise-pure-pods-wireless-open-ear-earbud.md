### Pros
- ✅ Comfortable open-ear design that does not cause ear canal pain during long sessions
- ✅ Versatile detachable Pure Band easily converts the earbuds into a secure neckband for workouts
- ✅ Excellent battery life with up to 80 hours of total playtime and quick charging
- ✅ IPX5 water resistance protects against sweat and outdoor splashes

### Cons
- ❌ Sound leakage is common due to the open-ear air conduction design
- ❌ Maximum volume levels can feel low or quiet, especially in noisy environments
- ❌ Frequent customer reports of Bluetooth pairing issues and rapid battery draining over time
- ❌ Inconsistent build quality with some units failing shortly after minimal use
