## Why We Fail to Predict Exponential Growth
- Linear Thinking: We intuitively project the future by looking backward linearly, failing to account for exponential acceleration.
- S-Curve Distortion: Progress occurs in S-curves (slow start, explosive growth, leveling off). Looking at a flat phase of an S-curve tricks us into thinking progress has stalled.
- Experience Bias: We base our predictions on personal history, dismissing radical future possibilities as unrealistic.

## The Paradox of Creating AGI
- Easy vs. Hard Tasks: Computers easily solve complex math and chess, but struggle with basic human tasks like visual recognition, spatial movement, and language comprehension.
- Evolutionary Optimization: Human skills like vision and motor control have been optimized over hundreds of millions of years of evolution, making them feel effortless to us but incredibly complex to program.
- Moravec's Paradox: AI has succeeded in doing what requires conscious 'thinking' but fails at what humans and animals do 'without thinking'.
