# Restaurant-Style Paneer Butter Masala

## Ingredients

**To saute & puree**
- 1 tablespoon (15 ml) oil
- 2 green cardamoms (or ¼ to ⅓ tsp ground cardamom)
- 1 cup (130 grams) onions, sliced or diced
- 1 inch (10 grams) ginger, peeled & sliced
- 5 to 6 (12 grams) large garlic cloves, peeled
- 2 cups (350 grams) tomatoes, sliced
- 15 (30 grams) whole cashew nuts
- 1 teaspoon sea salt
- ¾ to 1 teaspoon Kashmiri red chilli powder
- ¾ to 1 teaspoon garam masala powder
- 1 teaspoon coriander powder (optional)
- 1 cup (240 ml) cold water (to blend)

**To finish the dish**
- 2 tablespoons (30 grams) unsalted butter
- 1 small bay leaf (optional)
- 2 inch Ceylon cinnamon piece (optional)
- 3 cloves (optional)
- 250 to 300 grams (9 to 10 oz) paneer, cubed to ¾ inch
- 1 teaspoon jaggery or sugar (optional)
- ½ tablespoon kasuri methi, crushed
- 3 to 4 tablespoons (45 to 60 ml) heavy cream
- 1 to 2 tablespoon coriander leaves, finely chopped

## Method
1. Pour oil to a pan and add cardamoms, onions, ginger, and garlic. Saute for 4 to 5 minutes until the onions turn transparent.
2. Add tomatoes, salt, and cashews. Mix well and cook covered on medium heat for about 7 minutes until the tomatoes turn mushy and break down.
3. Stir in the red chili powder, coriander powder, and garam masala. Saute on medium heat for 3 to 4 minutes until aromatic, then turn off and let cool.
4. Pour 1 cup of cold water into a blender jar, transfer the cooled onion-tomato masala, and blend to a silky smooth puree.
5. On low heat, melt butter in the same pan and add the optional whole spices (bay leaf, cinnamon, and cloves) if using, letting them sizzle for 30 seconds.
6. Hold a strainer over the pan and pour the onion-tomato masala through it, stirring with a spoon to strain out any grit.
7. Pour 1/2 cup more water into the pan and mix well. Cook covered on medium heat for 10 to 12 minutes until slightly thick yet flowy, stirring every 3 to 4 minutes.
8. Remove and discard the whole spices. Taste and adjust salt and garam masala, then stir in the jaggery, kasuri methi, and paneer.
9. Cook covered for 2 to 3 minutes on low heat, then turn off the stove. Stir in the heavy cream, reserving one tablespoon for garnish.
10. Garnish with the reserved cream and chopped coriander leaves, and serve hot with naan, roti, or Basmati rice.

## Tips
- To make this recipe vegan, substitute paneer with extra firm tofu, and use vegan butter and vegan cream.
- Avoid overcooking paneer to prevent it from becoming rubbery; cook it briefly and let it sit in the residual heat of the covered pan.
- Straining the blended puree is highly recommended to achieve the signature silky-smooth restaurant texture.
- Store kasuri methi in the refrigerator so it remains crisp and easily crushable in your palms.
- If you do not have cashews, substitute them with overnight soaked almonds, almond flour, or poppy seeds.
