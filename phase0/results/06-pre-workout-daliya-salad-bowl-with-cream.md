## Ingredients

**For the Salad Base**
- 40 g Daliya (broken wheat)
- Water for boiling
- Sweet corn, boiled (to taste)
- 1 small Tomato, chopped
- 1/2 Capsicum, chopped
- 1/2 Onion, chopped
- Lettuce, chopped

**For the Creamy Dressing**
- 1 tsp Olive oil
- 5-6 Mushrooms, sliced
- 2-3 Garlic cloves, chopped
- 1/2 Onion, chopped
- 50 g Paneer, cubed
- Salt to taste
- Black pepper to taste

## Method
1. In a pan, boil 40g of daliya in water until it absorbs all the liquid and becomes completely dry.
2. Transfer the cooked daliya into a serving bowl.
3. Add the sweet corn, chopped tomato, capsicum, onion, and lettuce on top of the daliya.
4. In a separate pan, heat 1 tsp of olive oil.
5. Add the sliced mushrooms, chopped garlic, onion, and paneer cubes to the pan.
6. Season with salt and black pepper, then sauté the mixture well until cooked through.
7. Transfer the sautéed mushroom and paneer mixture into a blender jar and blend until it forms a smooth, creamy dressing.
8. Pour the warm creamy dressing over the salad bowl, mix thoroughly, and enjoy.

## Tips
- Consume this meal approximately 2 hours before your workout for sustained energy, strength, and a great muscle pump.
- Feel free to customize the bowl with any of your favorite seasonal vegetables.
