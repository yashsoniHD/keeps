| Renegade Row | — | — | 60s | 10s | Maintain a strong plank position with feet wide for stability. Row the dumbbell up while keeping hips square to the floor; do not let your torso rotate. |
| Bicycle Crunch | — | — | 60s | 10s | Bring opposite elbow to opposite knee. Keep shoulder blades lifted off the mat and engage your obliques throughout. |

## Tips
- If a weight feels too light to challenge you for the full 60 seconds, quickly swap it for a heavier one.
- Focus on slow, controlled movements rather than using momentum to swing the weights.
- Keep your core engaged and hips tucked under during standing exercises to protect your lower back.
