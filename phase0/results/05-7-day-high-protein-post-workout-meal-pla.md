## Ingredients

**Monday (38g Protein)**
- 150 ml milk with daliya
- 50 g soya chunks
- 20 g peanuts
- 1 banana
- 2 anjeer (figs)

**Tuesday (25g Protein)**
- 50 g moong sprouts
- 100 g paneer, cubed
- 1 banana
- 4 dates
- 2 anjeer (figs)

**Wednesday (29g Protein)**
- 1 banana peanut butter sandwich (2 slices of bread)
- 1 banana
- 3 whole eggs, boiled
- 8 almonds
- 4 dates

**Thursday (37g Protein)**
- 50 g soaked soya chunks
- 150 ml milk with 40g oats
- 1 banana
- 1 handful soaked walnuts
- 1 handful raisins

**Friday (23g Protein)**
- 50 g poha
- 8 to 10 almonds
- 100 g tofu, cubed
- 1 banana

**Saturday (31g Protein)**
- 50 g chana sprouts
- 1 apple
- 4 boiled eggs
- 2 anjeer (figs)

**Sunday (27g Protein)**
- 3 egg whites
- 50 g paneer, cubed
- 1 peanut butter sandwich
- 1 tsp raisins (kishmish)

## Method
1. Monday: Boil 50g soya chunks for 5 minutes. Serve alongside daliya cooked in 150ml milk, 20g peanuts, 1 banana, and 2 figs.
2. Tuesday: Combine 50g fresh moong sprouts with 100g cubed paneer. Serve with 1 banana, 4 dates, and 2 figs.
3. Wednesday: Prepare a peanut butter sandwich topped with banana slices. Serve with 3 boiled eggs, 1 banana, 8 almonds, and 4 dates.
4. Thursday: Soak 50g soya chunks. Prepare 40g oats in 150ml milk. Serve with 1 banana and a handful of soaked walnuts and raisins.
5. Friday: Prepare 50g of light poha. Serve alongside 100g cubed tofu, 8-10 almonds, and 1 banana.
6. Saturday: Serve 50g of black chana sprouts with 4 boiled eggs, 1 fresh apple, and 2 figs.
7. Sunday: Prepare 3 egg whites and 50g paneer. Serve with 1 peanut butter sandwich sprinkled with raisins.

## Tips
- To make sprouts: Soak whole moong or black chana for 12-14 hours, then drain and let them sprout.
- Always boil soya chunks for at least 5 minutes to make them soft and easily digestible.
- Perfect budget-friendly meal prep option for hostel students, PG residents, and busy office goers.
