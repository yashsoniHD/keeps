## Day 1: Tokyo: Modern Highlights & Imperial Grounds
- `09:00` **Shibuya Crossing & Harajuku** — Walk across the iconic busy crossing and wander the quirky fashion boutiques of Harajuku.
- `13:30` **Imperial Palace** — Watch the changing of the guard. Take a free walk around the East Gardens or join a 75-minute guided tour (runs Tuesday-Saturday at 10:00 AM and 1:30 PM).
- `16:00` **Ueno Park** — Stroll through the historic park, home to the Tokyo National Museum, a zoo, and thousands of cherry blossoms in April.

## Day 2: Tokyo: Fish Markets & Mount Fuji Views
- `04:00` **Toyosu & Tsukiji Fish Markets** — Watch the early morning tuna auctions at Toyosu, then head to the Tsukiji outer market starting at 06:00 AM for fresh sushi breakfast.
- `09:00` **Mount Fuji Day Trip** — Take a day tour from Tokyo (around 12,000 JPY) to view Japan's tallest mountain, or climb it if visiting during the July to mid-September season.

## Day 3: Nikko: Mountain Shrines & Waterfalls
- `08:00` **Nikko National Park** — Travel 2 hours north of Tokyo to see the Nikko Toshogu shrine, Kegon Falls, Ryuzu Falls, and the scenic Shinkyo Bridge.

## Day 4: Kyoto: Bamboo Forests & Geisha Districts
- `08:00` **Arashiyama Bamboo Forest** — Arrive early to beat the crowds and wander the towering bamboo pathways.
- `18:00` **Gion District** — Explore the historic Geisha district. Note that photography is forbidden in narrow alleys to prevent harassment of geishas.

## Day 5: Nara: Wild Deer & Ancient Temples
- `09:00` **Nara Park & Todai-ji** — Feed the 1,300 free-roaming wild deer with crackers, and visit Todai-ji, the world's largest wooden building dating back to the 8th century.

## Day 6: Osaka: Castles & Street Food
- `13:00` **Osaka Castle** — Explore the 1931 reconstructed castle, its museum, and the observation deck offering panoramic city views.
- `18:00` **Dotonbori Food Tour** — Sample local specialties like okonomiyaki (savory pancakes) and kushikatsu (skewered kebabs). Food tours cost around 12,000 JPY.

## Day 7: Hiroshima: Peace Memorials
- `10:00` **Hiroshima Peace Memorial (Genbaku Dome)** — Visit the sobering ruins of the dome and the Peace Memorial Museum to learn about the 1945 atomic bombing.

## Day 8: Miyajima: The Floating Torii
- `09:00` **Itsukushima Shrine** — Take a ferry to 'Shrine Island' to see the iconic floating torii gate. Hike Mount Misen or take the cable car for 2,000 JPY round-trip.

## Day 9: Kanazawa: Edo-Era Heritage
- `10:00` **Nagamachi Samurai District** — Wander through preserved samurai and geisha districts, often called 'Little Kyoto' but without the heavy crowds.
- `14:00` **DT Suzuki Museum** — Learn about Zen Buddhism at this peaceful museum dedicated to the philosopher who introduced Zen to the West.

## Day 10: Takashima: Lake Biwa & Hida Beef
- `10:00` **Shirahige Shrine** — Visit the picturesque floating torii gate on Lake Biwa, walk the cherry tree route, and try local Hida beef.

## Tips
- Get a JR Pass before arriving in Japan to save on expensive bullet trains (50,000 JPY for 7 days, 80,000 JPY for 14 days).
- Eat cheap at 7-Eleven, Family Mart, or 100-yen shops, and buy discounted supermarket food after 8:00 PM for up to 50% off.
- Use the Navitime app for offline maps, train routes, and transit schedules.
- Avoid tattoos at traditional onsen (hot springs) or ensure they are covered, as many bathhouses restrict them.
- Take long-distance highway buses (like Willer Express) starting at 3,700 JPY if you have more time than money.
