# Quick and Tasty Paneer Masala Gravy

> **AI summary:** A simple yet flavorful Indian paneer gravy made by pan-frying spiced paneer cubes and simmering them in a rich tomato-yogurt onion gravy. Perfect as a quick side dish for parotta, roti, chapati, or jeera rice.

## Ingredients

**For Frying Paneer**
- 200 g paneer, diced
- 3 tbsp cooking oil
- 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
- 1/4 tsp turmeric powder
- 1/2 tsp salt

**For the Gravy**
- 1 bay leaf
- 1/4 tsp fennel seeds (saunf)
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 2 cardamom pods
- 3 cloves
- 2 small onions, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1/4 tsp turmeric powder
- 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri red chilli powder
- 1 tbsp coriander powder
- 1/2 tsp garam masala powder
- 1 medium tomato, ground to a paste
- 1 tbsp curd (yogurt), ground with the tomato
- 1.25 cups water
- 1 tbsp coriander leaves, chopped
- Salt, to taste

## Method
1. Heat 3 tbsp oil in a pan. Add 1/2 tsp red chilli powder, 1/4 tsp turmeric powder, and 1/2 tsp salt.
2. Add 200g diced paneer cubes and fry until the outer layer is slightly crispy. Remove the paneer and set aside.
3. In the same oil, add 1 bay leaf, 1/4 tsp fennel seeds, 1 cinnamon stick, 2 cardamoms, and 3 cloves. Fry for a few seconds.
4. Add 2 finely chopped onions and sauté for 2 minutes until they change color.
5. Add salt to taste and 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste. Sauté well.
6. Add 1/4 tsp turmeric powder, 1/2 tsp red chilli powder, 1/2 tsp Kashmiri red chilli powder, 1 tbsp coriander powder, and 1/2 tsp garam masala. Mix well on low heat.
7. Grind 1 tomato with 1 tbsp curd (yogurt) and a little water into a smooth paste. Add this paste to the pan.
8. Cover and cook on medium flame for 2 minutes until oil separates.
9. Add 1 cup of water (plus a little more if needed) to adjust gravy consistency. Cover and simmer for 2-3 minutes.
10. Add the fried paneer cubes, mix gently, cover, and simmer on low heat for 1 minute.
11. Garnish with 1 tbsp chopped coriander leaves and serve hot.

## Tips
- Grind the tomato with 1 tbsp of curd (yogurt) for a rich, tangy flavor.
- You can substitute paneer with mushrooms, potatoes, or chicken using the same gravy base.
- Add kasuri methi or fresh cream at the end for an even richer restaurant-style taste.
