## Ingredients

**Bhel Base**
- 15 g makhana (fox nuts)
- 60 g boiled kala chana (black chickpeas)
- 1/2 onion, finely chopped
- 1/2 tomato, finely chopped

**Dressing & Spices**
- 1 tbsp green chutney
- 1/2 tbsp imli (tamarind) chutney, made with 0-calorie sweetener
- 50 g skyr or high-protein Greek yogurt
- Chaat masala, to taste
- Roasted jeera (cumin) powder, to taste
- Salt, to taste
- Lemon juice, to taste

**Garnish**
- 1 tsp crushed peanuts
- 1 tsp baked sev

## Method
1. Dry roast the makhana in a pan for 2-3 minutes until crunchy, then lightly crush them with your hands.
2. In a small bowl, whisk the green chutney and skyr (Greek yogurt) together until smooth.
3. In a large mixing bowl, combine the roasted makhana, boiled kala chana, chopped onion, and chopped tomato.
4. Add the yogurt-chutney mixture, imli chutney, chaat masala, roasted jeera powder, salt, and a squeeze of fresh lemon juice.
5. Mix everything thoroughly until well coated.
6. Top with crushed peanuts and baked sev, and serve immediately.

## Tips
- Serve immediately after mixing to prevent the roasted makhana from losing its crunch.
- Using skyr or Greek yogurt instead of traditional chutneys alone significantly boosts the protein content to 17g.
- Keep the calorie count low by preparing the tamarind chutney with a zero-calorie sweetener.
