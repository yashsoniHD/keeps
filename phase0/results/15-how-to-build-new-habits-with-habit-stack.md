# How to Build New Habits with Habit Stacking

> **AI summary:** This guide explains habit stacking, a method of linking new behaviors to established daily routines to build lasting habits. By leveraging the brain's natural synaptic pruning and existing neural pathways, you can create highly specific, actionable triggers for self-improvement.

## Basic Habit Stacking Examples
- After I pour my cup of coffee each morning, I will meditate for one minute.
- After I take off my work shoes, I will immediately change into my workout clothes.
- After I sit down to dinner, I will say one thing I’m grateful for.
- After I put on my running shoes, I will text a friend where I am running.

## Advanced Habit Chains
- Morning Stack: Coffee -> Meditate 60s -> Write to-do list -> Begin first task.
- Evening Stack: Finish dinner -> Plate in dishwasher -> Wipe counter -> Set out coffee mug.
- Routine Insertion: Wake up -> Make bed -> Place book on pillow -> Take a shower (ensures book is on pillow for bedtime).

## Situational Rules
- Stairs: When I see a set of stairs, I will take them instead of the elevator.
- Finances: When I want to buy something over $100, I will wait 24 hours before purchasing.
- Decluttering: When I buy a new item, I will give something away ('One in, one out').
- Mindfulness: When the phone rings, I will take one deep breath and smile before answering.

## Steps
1. Brainstorm your current daily habits (e.g., brushing teeth, brewing coffee, closing your laptop).
2. Identify events that happen to you daily without fail (e.g., receiving a text, a song ending).
3. Select a highly specific, unambiguous trigger (e.g., 'When I close my laptop for lunch' instead of 'At lunch').
4. Ensure the trigger matches the frequency of the new habit (e.g., daily triggers for daily habits).
5. Write your stack using the formula: After/Before [CURRENT HABIT], I will [NEW HABIT].
